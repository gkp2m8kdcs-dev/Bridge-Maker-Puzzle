Privacy Policy

Bridge Maker Puzzle · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Bridge Maker Puzzle ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, how that information is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the terms set out below.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any personal information from you. Specifically:

  • No account, email, or password is required to use the App.
  • No usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking SDKs are integrated.
  • No data is transmitted to remote servers operated by the developer.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising networks are used.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data exclusively on your iPhone:

  • Game records: wins, losses, swaps used, duration, mode, and the seed behind each puzzle.
  • Custom puzzles: up to five saved bridges, including any card art you provide.
  • Settings: preferences for haptic feedback, sounds, and auto-save.
  • Photos you choose: if you opt to design your own puzzles in Bridge Workshop, the photos you pick from your library or capture with the camera are saved locally for use as card art.

All of this data stays on this device. It is not uploaded, synced, or shared with anyone by the App. You can remove all stored data at any time from the in-app Settings screen.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to capture a photo for a custom puzzle in Bridge Workshop. The App does not record video, and the camera is never accessed in the background.
  • Photo Library: used only when you choose to pick an existing photo for a custom puzzle in Bridge Workshop. The App requests the minimum level of access necessary.

You may deny either permission and still enjoy the rest of Bridge Maker Puzzle without designing custom puzzles.

Children's Privacy

The App does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from any user. Because nothing leaves your device, the App is suitable for family use.

Data Security

Because no information leaves your device, the security of your data depends on the security of your iPhone. We recommend keeping your device passcode enabled and your iOS version up to date.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page along with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted within the App where appropriate.
