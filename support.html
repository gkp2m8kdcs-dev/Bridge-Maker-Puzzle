Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an account?

No. Bridge Maker Puzzle is fully offline and does not require login, signup, or any account.

Where is my game data stored?

Everything stays on your iPhone — game records, custom puzzles, photos, and settings. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

How do I wipe my data?

Open the in-app Settings screen and use the wipe action to clear all records, photos, and saved bridges.

Why is the camera or photo library permission requested?

Only when you want to design a custom puzzle in Bridge Workshop. The App does not access your camera or photos in the background.

What devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later, in portrait orientation.

Report a Bug

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The App version (found on the Settings screen).
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • Screenshots, if possible.
