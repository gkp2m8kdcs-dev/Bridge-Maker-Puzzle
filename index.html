Bridge Maker Puzzle

Build Toy Bridges. Swap the Cards. Watch the Car Roll.

About the App

Bridge Maker Puzzle is a single-device puzzle game about the quiet joy of finishing a toy bridge. Every round starts with a grid of image cards, each holding a color and a height value. Tap one card, then tap a neighbor to swap them. When three or more cards of the same color line up along a row or column, they bind into an arch, glowing softly to show the connection. Once an edge-to-edge arch closes, a gentle path is drawn across the board with rounded line segments. When every required bridge is closed, a small toy car rolls across your finished crossing, with haptics marking every milestone along the way.

Four Modes, Every Pace

Skyline Studio

Two players pass the iPhone back and forth; whoever closes the last bridge wins the round.

Stream Camp

A casual, timer-free sandbox for a slow afternoon by the water — great for practicing tricky swaps.

Bridge Workshop

Bring your own photos and design up to five custom puzzles, replayable any time.

Daily Crossing

A fresh seeded puzzle every day so a quick session always feels new, with streak rewards.

What's Inside

  • Custom card art captured with the camera or picked from your photo library.
  • Up to five saved bridges, each renameable, deletable, and replayable.
  • Transit Records tab: full history of every crossing — wins, losses, swaps used, duration, and the seed behind the puzzle — plus filters by mode.
  • Settings for haptic feedback, sounds, and auto-save, with a one-tap wipe for a fresh start.
  • No login, no signup, no account, no server. Every record, photo, and bridge stays on your iPhone.

Requirements

Bridge Maker Puzzle runs on iPhone with iOS 16 or later, in portrait only. The app is fully offline and ready for the App Store today.
